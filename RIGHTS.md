This public repository provides documentation, translation resources, collaboration configuration, and an independently authored external adapter example. No general open-source license has been applied to these materials; their public availability should not be read as an open-source license for the product or the repository contents. The Obsidian example follows this same rights-retained policy and is not an Obsidian official plugin or a distribution of Jotra product source.

Submit material you authored or are authorized to contribute. If your contribution has special reuse conditions, state them in the issue or pull request so they can be confirmed before incorporation. Authors of any future external integration must specify the rights and license for their own work separately.
